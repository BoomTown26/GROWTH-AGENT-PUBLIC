Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Growth agent is a private tool used only by its owner to analyse the owner's own video channels. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

Data accessed

  • Analytics for the owner's own YouTube channels and videos (for example views, watch time, audience retention and aggregated audience age, gender and country), through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, with read-only permission.
  • Public comments on the owner's own videos. These are read only to produce aggregated topic summaries.

How it is used

  • To measure how the owner's videos perform and to plan and test new videos.
  • Only aggregated numbers are kept (for example totals per video). No information about individual viewers is collected or kept.
  • Comment text is processed in memory to produce summaries and is not stored. Names, usernames, links and contact details in comments are removed before any processing.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored on the owner's own computer.
  • It is not sold, rented or shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.
  • Comment text may be sent to an AI model provider solely to produce the topic summaries; it is not retained by the tool afterwards.

Use of Google data

Growth agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, Growth agent can no longer read any data.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
