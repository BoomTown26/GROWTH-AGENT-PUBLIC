Growth agent

Growth agent is a private tool that helps its owner run their own video channels. It reads analytics and public comment counts from channels the owner controls, compares how videos perform, and suggests what to make and test next.

It is not a public service: only the owner uses it, and only for their own channels.

What it accesses

  • YouTube channel and video analytics for the owner's own channels, read-only (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Public comments on the owner's own videos, summarised into topics. Individual comments are not stored.

It never uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything on YouTube.
